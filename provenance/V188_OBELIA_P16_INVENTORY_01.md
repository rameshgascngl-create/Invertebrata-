## Frozen instructional inventory
Three organism-level independent requirements are frozen:
1. OBELIA-01 — colony architecture with independently readable feeding/reproductive zooid treatment.
2. OBELIA-02 — medusa anatomy.
3. OBELIA-03 — life cycle / metagenesis process.

Required subfigure obligations, not separate organism-level requirements:
- OBELIA-01A — hydranth/gastrozooid detail.
- OBELIA-01B — gonangium/gonozooid detail.
- OBELIA-03A — planula as a readable life-cycle stage.

Polymorphism is NOT REQUIRED AS INDEPENDENT FIGURE; it is satisfied only if OBELIA-01/01A/01B visibly distinguish feeding and reproductive zooids and their functions.

This inventory freeze precedes candidate judgment. No SVG was modified or created.
