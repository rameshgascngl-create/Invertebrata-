## Findings and implemented corrections

| Topic | Finding / correction | Limit or review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrangement | Longitudinal cortical rows; cortical units can have one or two basal bodies; not every basal body is ciliated. | Eleven representative ciliated units do not imply an actual count or complete cortical map. |
| Basal body | Old dots lay at the surface line and did not distinguish ultrastructure. Now bodies are drawn beneath the surface, with an enlarged nine-triplet inset. | Triplets, hub/rootlets and docking are simplified; terminal/intermediate/axosomal plates are omitted. |
| Axoneme | Nine peripheral doublets and two central singlets are retained and enlarged. A complete A-tubule and incomplete shared-wall B-tubule are explained; central singlets are not doublets. | Axonemal shaft, basal body and ciliary tip are different structures. Protein complexes/protofilaments and molecular scales are omitted. |
| Recovery | Same-height, oppositely leaning curves understated the asymmetric recovery path. Recovery now curls nearer the cortex. | Two-dimensional projection cannot reproduce out-of-plane recovery. Source species/viscosity matter. |
| Effective stroke | Written text identifies mainly posterior fluid displacement during forward swimming and ATP-dependent dynein/sliding/bending. | No measured beat angle, beat frequency or calibrated fluid velocity. |
| Metachrony | Stage 4 displays neighbouring phase differences; an activity wave does not mean cilia or basal bodies migrate. | Selected colouring is a teaching highlight, not a leader cilium. Four views are teaching views, not four sequential physiological events. |
| Avoidance | Added specific depolarization → ciliary Ca2+ entry → effective-stroke reversal → short backward swim → recovery/reorientation. Posterior stimulation can accelerate forward motion. | Ion mechanisms require physiological evidence; the drawing is not a calcium recording. |
| Observation | Added explicit light-microscope versus electron-microscope versus physiological-evidence distinctions. | Ordinary classroom light microscopy cannot establish a 9+2 cross-section. |

## Consulted authoritative material and access record

1. **Tamm, S. L. (1972). Ciliary motion in Paramecium: A scanning electron microscope study. J Cell Biol 55:250–255. DOI 10.1083/jcb.55.1.250.** Primary SEM study, *P. multimicronucleatum*. Retrieved full PDF text, especially Results and Figures 2–4 captions, through the research search service. Describes three-dimensional, asymmetric recovery near the surface and viscosity dependence. Original PMC metadata: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2108756/ . Readable paper: https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/1d0a/9d4b552323c7ae010ac9bd1384d9bdf6b338.pdf . The local PDF mirror transfer did not produce usable bytes; no claim of locally inspected SEM pixels is made.
2. **Bengueddach et al. (2017). Basal body positioning and anchoring in the multiciliated cell Paramecium tetraurelia: roles of OFD1 and VFL3. Cilia 6:6. DOI 10.1186/s13630-017-0050-z.** Primary localization/ultrastructure experiments. Full source text consulted; original Figure 2 TEM image downloaded and visually inspected, separating normal WT from OFD1-depleted specimens. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13630-017-0050-z . The WT panel supports docking beneath the surface through the transition zone; mutant panels must not be treated as normal anatomy.
3. **Lodh et al. (2016). Voltage-gated calcium channels of Paramecium cilia. J Exp Biol 219:3028–3038. DOI 10.1242/jeb.141234.** Primary RNAi/channel-localization/Pawn-rescue experiments in *P. tetraurelia*. Full source retrieved and consulted, abstract and channel-function evidence. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5091652/ . Supports ciliary calcium entry and reversal; does not certify *P. caudatum* kinetics.
4. **Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th ed., Molecular Motors.** Established cell-biology reference for shared-wall doublets, dynein and constrained sliding. Authoritative publisher-authorized NCBI excerpts consulted; whole-page fetch presented an access challenge. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26888/ .
5. **Brette (2021), Integrative Neuroscience of Paramecium, eNeuro, DOI 10.1523/ENEURO.0018-21.2021.** Existing scholarly synthesis consulted to trace primary references and contrast anterior/posterior responses. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8208649/ . Claims were checked against the primary studies above rather than treated as new experimental measurements.

No source photograph was copied/traced into the Android illustration. The candidate remains original Kotlin Canvas graphite artwork. Source access/figure consultation is distinct from permission to redistribute a photograph.
