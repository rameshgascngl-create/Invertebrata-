## Anatomical and physiological audit

- Whole-cell inset locates two complexes. Both locator symbols stay static; only one enlarged representative complex is animated. No synchronized discharge claim is made.
- Reservoir, proximal ampulla, collecting canal, smooth tubules, peripheral decorated tubules and cortical pore have distinct keys and touch geometry. The separate lower-right comparative inset is enlarged independently.
- The visible radial-arm count is representative. The drawing supplies neither a measured cell scale nor a species-constant number of arms.
- Discontinuity marks omit changing connections. The outlet route, pore state, lumen fraction and blue flow cues are explicitly schematic. Fine recovery-network reconstruction, measured membrane transport, universal salt response and molecular trajectories are outside scope.
- The four existing semantic stages are retained: osmotic entry, collection, filling/diastole, discharge/systole. Events can overlap in life. Three-second presentation intervals are illustrative, not experimental timings. Discharge decreases the lumen; playback stops at the final view, and replay is explicit.
- Osmoregulation is distinguished from digestion and food-vacuole egestion. Water movement is distinguished from energy-dependent ion transport. A changing vacuole outline does not demonstrate an encasing muscle.
- Light-microscope observation, electron-microscope structure and physiological inference are distinguished in every relevant subsection. A suitable wet mount does not resolve individual pumps or ion gradients.

Unresolved specialist questions: whether the chosen radial-arm and cortex proportions are sufficiently instructive; whether more recovery-network geometry should be added after full-paper review; which Tamil equivalents the department prefers for spongiome and ampulla. These are pending review, not certified accurate artwork.
