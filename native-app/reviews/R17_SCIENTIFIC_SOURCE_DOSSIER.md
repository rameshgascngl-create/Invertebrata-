## Consulted primary evidence

- Taka, Kurokawa, Araki & Mikami (2006), DOI 10.1111/j.1550-7408.2006.00091.x, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16677339/ : indexed primary abstract consulted. Usually one vegetative P. caudatum MIC; division fate and disappearance of extra presumptive germline products are separate. Full figures not accessed.
- Nakajima, Ishida & Mikami (2002), DOI 10.1111/j.1550-7408.2002.tb00344.x, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1550-7408.2002.tb00344.x : publisher abstract/authorship retrieved. One postmeiotic nucleus survives at the paroral region; it forms stationary and migratory pronuclei. Reciprocal exchange precedes fusion. Antibody/microinjection experiments support microtubule involvement. Original full figures remain access-limited.
- Yang & Takahashi (1999), DOI 10.1111/j.1550-7408.1999.tb04583.x, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10188260/ : authoritative abstract consulted during the R1.7 specification and indexed recheck. Third postzygotic division yields eight nuclei; positional differentiation must not be confused with immediate degradation. Heat-shocked abnormal cells are not normal teaching anatomy.
- Kimura, Mikami & Endoh (2004), DOI 10.1002/gene.20060, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15354289/ : full authoritative abstract retrieved. Old-MAC fragmentation is not instantaneous DNA degradation; parental fragments can persist across later cell cycles while the new MAC develops. Numerical timing is experiment-specific, not demonstration speed.
- Tucker, Beisson, Roche & Cohen (1980), DOI 10.1242/jcs.44.1.135, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7440651/ : full authoritative abstract retrieved. Primary P. tetraurelia microscopy demonstrates organized intranuclear microtubules and challenges a literal 'amitosis' description. Corrected R1.7 wording to 'traditionally called amitosis'; no claim of absence of microtubules or a featureless cut. Do not transfer its dimensions/kinetics to P. caudatum.

(2008), DOI 10.1101/gr.074534.107, https://genome.cshlp.org/content/18/4/585 : primary P. tetraurelia sequencing/source excerpts consulted in the specification; quantitative copy numbers and two-MIC vegetative anatomy are not P. caudatum constants. ## Illustration and terminology limits

The whole-cell plate is a horizontal schematic with anterior left, posterior right, a larger hatched MAC and compact MIC. Independently enlarged insets are not additional nuclei. Nuclear location, indentation, cilia density, chromatin marks, chromosome symbols and relative sizes are not calibrated measurements. Chromosome cartoons will use representative homolog/sister symbols, not an invented species karyotype. MAC/MIC are somatic/germline compartments, never male/female nuclei.
