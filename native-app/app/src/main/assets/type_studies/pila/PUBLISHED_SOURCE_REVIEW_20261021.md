# Pila published-figure sourcing review — 2026-10-21

Status: **REVIEW ONLY — NOT APPROVED FOR STUDENT ANATOMICAL PLATE SLOTS**.

The native gallery requires four separate plates: `external`, `mantle-cavity`, `ctenidium`, and `torsion`. A shell photograph cannot establish mantle-cavity anatomy.

## Published source candidates and decisions

1. **William Swainson, *Zoological Illustrations*, vol. II, plate 103 (1821)** — historical illustration of *Ampullaria fasciata*, catalogued by Wikimedia Commons as *Pila ampullacea*. Wikimedia Commons file page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zoological_Illustrations_Volume_II_Plate_103.jpg . **Public domain** according to the file page. Potential **supplementary species-comparative external-shell illustration only**; it is not *Pila globosa* and does not show mantle organs. Do not insert into `pila/external.png` without a clearly identified species-specific scope and faculty acceptance.
2. **Al2, “Scheme snail anatomy-numbers.svg” (2007)** — Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scheme_snail_anatomy-numbers.svg . **CC BY 3.0** (also offered under GFDL). The subject is a common terrestrial snail, not *Pila*; depicted reproductive structures and respiratory arrangement must not be transferred to the amphibious ampullariid. **REJECT for all four Pila plate slots**.
3. **Internet Archive Book Images, “Snail, diagrammatic side view.jpg,” from *Animal Biology* (1938)** — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Snail,_diagrammatic_side_view.jpg . File page marks **CC0 1.0**, but this is a generic snail digestive schematic, not *Pila* mantle-cavity or ctenidium anatomy. **REJECT for required Pila plate slots**.
4. **NIScPR/NSDL, “PHYLUM MOLLUSCA” teaching PDF** — https://nsdl.niscpr.res.in/bitstream/123456789/693/1/PHYLUM%20MOLLUSCA%20-%20Formatted.pdf . Contains labelled *Pila* figures including “Body of Pila after removing the shell” and “Pila: Mantle cavity and pallial complex,” identifying pulmonary sac/chamber, branchial chamber, ctenidium, epitaenia, rectum and anus. **Scientifically relevant lead; reuse rights not established**. Do not copy images into repository or APK without explicit compatible licensing/permission.
5. **Naturalis Biodiversity Center specimen ZMA.MOLL.318132, *Pila globosa*** — https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Naturalis_Biodiversity_Center_-_ZMA.MOLL.318132_-_Pila_globosa_Swainson_-_Ampullariidae_-_Mollusc_shell.jpeg . **CC0 1.0**. Already integrated as a **supplementary museum shell photograph**; not a substitute for any required anatomical plate.

## Acceptance checklist for each replacement

- Correct species or explicit genus-level anatomical scope, with no misleading labels.
- Verify structure and leader-line endpoints against an independent zoological source; preserve the published figure's original anatomy.
- Record author, year, title, publisher/institution, source URL, exact license URL, modification details and downloaded-file checksum.
- Where attribution or share-alike terms apply, include them in the app and repository.
- Retain missing-plate indicators until an actual raster master is committed and independently reviewed.
- Verify raster decoding, fit/enlargement, English–Tamil captions and Android screenshot evidence before release.

**No anatomical figure was downloaded or inserted by this review. Release HOLD remains.**


## Additional source comparison — 21 October 2026

**Editorial decision:** retain the two already bundled and credited images:
- `shell-specimen-reference.png`: Naturalis *Pila globosa* specimen, CC0, supplementary shell reference.
- `swainson-1821-pila-ampullacea.png`: historical *Pila ampullacea* comparative plate, public domain; not *P. globosa*.

Additional candidates reviewed:

Original 1505 x 2585 JPEG; Commons US public-domain notice, international status must be verified. **Shell/external comparison only**, not mantle-cavity or ctenidium anatomy.

3. Published *Pila globosa* female pallial-cavity figure (caption Fig. 60.6) showing ctenidium, osphradium and pulmonary sac: https://player.uacdn.net/slides_pdf/6DJRFIMZYX0K3Y5O7X2X/Mollusca_Feeding_Respiration_Locomotion_General_Features__Life_History_of_Lamellidens__Pila_with_anno.pdf . **Scientifically relevant candidate, redistribution rights unverified; do not import**.

**Decision:** no new figure is superior to the committed CC0 shell specimen for shell-only teaching. Keep existing imagery; do not replace, duplicate, or mislabel. The four required Pila anatomy slots remain unaccepted until species-appropriate figures and permissions are verified.
