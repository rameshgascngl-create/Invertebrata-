## Reuse decision
The earlier figures **are recoverable** and should be reviewed before requesting replacements. However, filename alone is not proof of species identity, scientific accuracy, copyright clearance or legible labels. Do not promote the old `04_animal_front.png` or `08_respiratory_system.png` to the mandatory `type_studies/pila/` anatomical slots without inspecting their actual pixels and attribution. The existing CC0 museum specimen remains the approved supplementary shell reference.
