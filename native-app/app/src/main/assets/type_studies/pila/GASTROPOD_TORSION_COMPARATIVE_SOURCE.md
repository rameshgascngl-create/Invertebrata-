# Gastropod torsion — published comparative source (NOT Pila-specific)

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica, 11th edition, Gastropoda (1911), p. 506, figure showing three developmental stages of *Trochus* torsion (after Robert).
Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EB1911-Gastropoda_1.png
Original file: 993 × 376 PNG. Commons describes it as public domain in the United States; international reuse status must be assessed before distribution in India.

## Scientific scope
- The figure illustrates developmental torsion in *Trochus*, not *Pila globosa*.
- Labels in the historical source include foot, operculum, pallial cavity and velum.
- It may support a **general gastropod torsion comparison** in the Unit V teaching text, with the source taxon clearly named.
- It MUST NOT be imported as the required `pila/torsion` species-specific anatomical master or counted as acceptance of that slot.
- The alternative 1911 Gastropoda figure 3 is a *model* of visceral-hump rotation, not a dissection of *Pila*: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EB1911-Gastropoda_3.png
