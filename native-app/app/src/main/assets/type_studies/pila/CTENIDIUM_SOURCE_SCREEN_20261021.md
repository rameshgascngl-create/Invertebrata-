## Editorial disposition: comparative reference only — NOT APPROVED for pila/ctenidium
The figure is suitable for a labelled comparison of molluscan ctenidia, but its Paludina panel must not be relabelled as the gill of Pila globosa. It cannot satisfy the prescribed Pila-specific ctenidium, osphradium and mantle-cavity anatomical plate. No source image has been imported or modified in this screening.
