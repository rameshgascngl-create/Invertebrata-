## Global defects

- Source is a 1536×1024 multi-panel composite, not 16 independently drawn figures. Crops range from 169×402 to 354×325 pixels (some Asterias crops are 283 pixels tall).
- Multiple files contain adjacent-panel remnants and clipped bilingual labels; text cannot be trusted as a source of terminology.
- Tamil wording and leader-line endpoints require independent editorial checks against Tamil Nadu Zoology textbook terminology.
- Replacing plates with accurate independently sourced or newly verified high-resolution images is preferred over interpolation or artificial enlargement.
- Each approved replacement needs source/provenance, scientific review, Tamil review, production inventory registration and a correct lesson reference.
- Device QA and APK optimisation remain deferred.
