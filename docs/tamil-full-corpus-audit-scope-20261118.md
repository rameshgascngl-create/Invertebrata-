## Chapter #24 — Mollusca, General Characters and Classification (19 November 2026)

- **General body plan:** a distinct head and locomotory foot are characteristic of the generalised molluscan plan but may be modified/reduced (e.g., Bivalvia and specialised aplacophorans). Clarified gastropods may have absent/reduced shells, not universally 'single, coiled' shells. Reference: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/28-4-superphylum-lophotrochozoa-mollusks-and-annelids .
- **Shell structure:** a periostracum and mineralised layers occur in many molluscan shells, but a nacreous inner layer is **not universal** and layer numbers/microstructures vary. Corrected main text and glossary in Tamil/English. Evidence: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7557267/ .
- **Development:** not all marine molluscs undergo a free trochophore → veliger sequence, nor is veliger always the 'second stage'. Cephalopods have direct development; some bivalves/gastropods have trochophore/veliger, shortened or alternate sequences. The crystalline style is present in many but not all bivalves. Ref: https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781351115650_A38583759/preview-9781351115650_A38583759.pdf .
- **Pearl formation:** mantle epithelial cells proliferate as a pearl sac following natural tissue displacement/injury or deliberate mantle grafting; a sand grain is **not** a necessary pearl nucleus. Common marine nucleated methods introduce a shell bead + mantle graft, while some freshwater methods use tissue-only grafts. Corrected pearl paragraph, pearl-sac glossary and both English/Tamil culture explanations. References: FAO https://www.fao.org/4/ab726e/ab726e11.htm ; experimental pearl study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4632584/ .
- **Feeding:** radula absent in Bivalvia, but not all bivalves are filter feeders; some are carnivorous. Corrected glossary/class comparison and shell-definition note without changing syllabus categories.
