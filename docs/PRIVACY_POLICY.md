# INVERTEBRATA — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026  
**App:** INVERTEBRATA — undergraduate Invertebrate Zoology textbook  
**Android package:** `com.gasczoology.invertebratelab`  
**Developer:** R. Ramesh, Department of Zoology, Government Arts and Science College, Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu, India

## What this app does
INVERTEBRATA is an educational Android application with English and Tamil textbook lessons, scientific illustrations and learning activities. Its core learning content works without an internet connection. There is no account registration, login or payment feature.

## Data collection and sharing
The application does **not** request Android's Internet permission and does not upload student learning records, device identifiers, contact details or personal information to the developer's servers. The app does not integrate an advertising or analytics service. It does not sell or share user data for advertising.

## Information stored on your device
To support learning, the application saves the selected language, reading progress and reading position, bookmarks, and learning activity state locally on your Android device. These records stay in the app's private device storage. They are not transmitted by the app to the developer.

## Speech and Android services
If you choose optional read-aloud functionality, the application invokes the speech engine installed on your device. The Android operating system and any independently installed text-to-speech provider may have their own data practices and privacy policies. Users can review the speech engine's settings and privacy terms separately.

## Data retention and deletion
Locally saved learning preferences and progress remain until you clear this app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android's device-data management. INVERTEBRATA disables Android automatic app backup. The developer has no remote account or server copy of these learning records to retrieve or delete.

## Children and students
The application is intended for education and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or students. No registration or personal details are required for study.

## External platforms
The app store, operating system, and any optional text-to-speech provider are separate services with their own privacy practices. Those services are not controlled by this application.

## Changes to this policy
Any material change to the app's data practices will require an updated version of this notice and, where relevant, updated app-store disclosures.

## Contact
For questions about the application's privacy or accessibility, use the project's public issue tracker:  
https://github.com/rameshgascngl-create/Invertebrata-/issues

Please do **not** include personal or sensitive information in a public GitHub issue. For private support, use the developer contact details shown on the app-store listing.
