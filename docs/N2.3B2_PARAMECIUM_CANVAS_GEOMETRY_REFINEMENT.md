## Source problem corrected

N2.3B1 scaled 1000x600 reference coordinates with different horizontal and
vertical factors, which stretched the cell depending on screen shape. Touch
coordinates were compared in normalized canvas axes, causing a mismatch between
drawn landmarks and the selection model after resizing or letterboxing.

N2.3B2 introduces one pure-Kotlin fitted viewport with a **single scale**,
centered margins and a defined inverse pixel-to-reference map. The same
viewport now governs the native Compose Canvas drawing and touch input.
Empty letterbox areas can never masquerade as anatomical hotspots.
Selections use the same undistorted reference-space distances as the art.

The initial trichocyst rods are moved to an explicitly *provisional*
subpellicular/cortical region rather than the cell's deep interior. Their
illustrated count, angle, thickness and coordinates remain schematic.
The five accessible buttons and bilingual labels remain unchanged.
The draft plate's pending-geometry and pending-touch identifiers are
deliberately unchanged; no review approval has been inferred.

   Modern Model Organism" (2023): Figure 1 illustrates light-microscopic
   P. caudatum, cilia, peristome, trichocysts and other anatomy.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10143506/
2. Beisson et al., "Preformed cell structure and cell heredity":
   cortical units, antero-posterior polarity, oral apparatus and cytoproct
   on the ventral meridian. This source's detailed cortical illustration
   is for P. tetraurelia, and is NOT a P. caudatum coordinate template.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2634414/
3. Scientific morphology review: P. caudatum body asymmetry, oral
   groove and posterior taper need matching to the actual orientation
   of any source specimen. The source's published figures are NOT copied.
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/agricultural-and-biological-sciences/paramecia
