## Correction to N2.3B3 source scope

The N2.3B3 cytoproct note used a *P. tetraurelia* comparator because an
unambiguous species-specific source had not yet been established. That
missing source is NOW FOUND — do not leave the academic record asserting
that this evidence is unavailable.

- Allen, R.D. and Wolf, R.W. (1974), *The cytoproct of Paramecium
  caudatum: structure and function, microtubules, and fate of food
  vacuole membranes*. Journal of Cell Science 14(3):611–631.
  PMID 4364579. DOI 10.1242/jcs.14.3.611.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4364579/
- Cell Image Library **CIL:39181**, Richard Allen, *P. caudatum*
  cytoproct in TEM. DOI 10.7295/W9CIL39181.
  https://www.cellimagelibrary.org/images/39181
- Richard Allen's Paramecium caudatum microscopy atlas, Chapter 10a,
  Figures **43–45**: cytoproct and nearby digestive vacuoles,
  microtubules and cytoproct ridge.
  https://www6.pbrc.hawaii.edu/allen/ch10a/
- Cell Image Library **CIL:36755**, a *P. caudatum* cross section
  showing a sub-surface layer of trichocysts. DOI 10.7295/W9CIL36755.
  https://www.cellimagelibrary.org/images/36755

These are species-authenticated and provide *qualitative anatomical
relationships*: the cytoproct ridge follows the posterior suture on
the ventral face, posterior to the opening of the oral apparatus, and
the trichocysts lie in the cortex beneath the membrane.

**Important limits:** transmission electron micrographs show sections
and cannot supply the Canvas's exact 2D whole-cell marker coordinates.
The published figures are not copied, traced or installed as app assets.
The exact (776,420) cytoproct marker and other fitted Canvas positions
are still unverified schematic placeholders. The research source solves
the *species identity* gap, NOT the *geometry approval* gap.

## Immutable review protocol

New Kotlin model ParameciumExternalReviewGate holds five review lines
whose IDs must match the authoritative external-cilia plate. Each line
requires exact source-figure location, species ID, geometry comparison
evidence, signed biological review and signed Tamil-term review.

Separately, an orientation check needs an identified *P. caudatum*
reference and human reviewer; a **physical Android device** review needs
portrait and landscape captures, Tamil 200% text proof, TalkBack proof,
hit-region behaviour proof, model and Android version.

The starting review packet intentionally has no approved review items.
Automated structural completeness of a packet only yields
"ready for independent human approval consideration" and never changes
NativeAnatomyPlate.status. The deployed plate remains DRAFT_UNVERIFIED.
Unit tests use obviously synthetic fixture values, never fake approvers
in the production data.
