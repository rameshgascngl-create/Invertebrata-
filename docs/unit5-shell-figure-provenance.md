# Unit V · Existing Pila shell images restored as supplementary references

Restoration decision: reuse three existing project PNGs **without modifying any pixel**.
These are teaching illustrations, **not** approved mandatory Pila anatomical master plates.
No third-party Wikimedia/CC licence is claimed for these images; they were already in
this repository as owner-directed, project-origin artwork. The original generation
or upload record, not a guessed external licence, governs downstream attribution.

**Source and limitations:** each original is 384 × 341 pixels. These may look soft
when enlarged; no artificial upscaling or new generation was performed.
The first and third images contain the source terms “ventral” and “dorsal”
for shell views. Their native English–Tamil captions explicitly use aperture,
apex, spire and whorl terminology to avoid teaching those labels as definitive
shell orientation. These were visually screened for limited shell teaching use,
not independently verified against an identified preserved specimen.

The other nine Pila figures and all four Asterias figures remain in
`src/debug/assets/unit5/candidates` only, due to unresolved organ topology,
wrong or duplicated labels, image orientation or unavailable source-pixel review.
The original 16-file debug archive is preserved byte-for-byte. No image is
promoted into `src/main/assets/type_studies`; the mandatory plate status
remains `DRAFT_UNVERIFIED`.

**Future replacement rule:** when a substantially wrong figure is replaced by
a published plate, record creator, title, source URL, exact licence version,
licence URL, modification notice and retrieval date; verify both biological
subject identity and redistribution permissions before bundling the bytes.
