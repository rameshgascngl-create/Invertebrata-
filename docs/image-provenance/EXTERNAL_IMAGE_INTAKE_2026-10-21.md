## Mandatory release gates
1. Inventory committed PNGs and compare image-by-image against the corresponding chapter before declaring any image missing.
2. Review morphology, organ topology, leader-line endpoints, stage chronology and English/Tamil scientific terminology against authoritative zoological sources. Record reviewer and evidence.
3. Record original creator, copyright holder, exact source URL, licence URL/version, retrieval date, original filename, original and adapted SHA-256, modification details, and attribution text.
4. Obtain explicit permission for all-rights-reserved works; credit alone is insufficient. For CC BY-SA adaptations, meet share-alike requirements for the adapted illustration and include the required licence notice.
5. Only after approval, produce a legible PNG raster at a suitable resolution and integrate into the native chapter. Do not use Canvas, synthetic placeholder anatomy, or unrelated images.
6. Exact-commit CI, screenshot review at 100% and 200% Tamil font scales, offline check, and physical-device acceptance remain distinct gates.
