## Scope delivered

A substantive, entirely native Kotlin English-Tamil draft lesson for
A5 chapter u1-paramecium. This is a scientific/editorial review draft;
it is NOT approved course material and NOT a completed nine-type-study app.

The draft has seven teaching sections:

1. Identity and habitat; historical Protozoa versus modern taxonomy.
2. Pellicle, ciliary motion, metachronal waves and avoidance response.
3. Oral groove, food vacuoles, intracellular digestion, cytoproct.
4. Contractile vacuoles and freshwater osmoregulation.
5. Macronucleus, micronucleus and transverse binary fission.
6. Conjugation, genetic exchange and nuclear reorganization.
7. Microscopy observations and evidence-based classroom questions.

NativeLessonDrafts uses typed NativeBilingualLesson and
BilingualLessonContract.validate from N2.1. ChapterScreen shows a visibly
labelled EN/TA editorial-draft warning and each section is rendered in
a separate lazy-list item for mobile scrolling. The validated A5 questions
and answer points remain unchanged.
