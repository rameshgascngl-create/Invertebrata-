# INVERTEBRATA N2.3A — Paramecium anatomy specification (draft)

Status: **SPECIFICATION / SOURCE REGISTER ONLY — NO CANVAS ACCEPTANCE**

## Scientific scope

Reference organism: *Paramecium caudatum*, to avoid falsely presenting
species-variable anatomy as universal for every *Paramecium* species.
The A5 question bank and N2.2 bilingual teaching prose remain unchanged.

Four required native plate specifications, all DRAFT_UNVERIFIED:

| Plate | Candidate labelled structures |
| --- | --- |
| external-cilia | pellicle, somatic cilia, oral groove, cytoproct, trichocysts |
| oral-apparatus | oral groove/peristome, vestibule, cytostome, cytopharynx, nascent food vacuole |
| contractile-vacuoles | anterior/posterior vacuoles, corresponding collecting canals |
| locomotion | somatic cilia, ciliary rows, basal bodies and pellicle |

The Kotlin model supplies distinct and stable organ IDs, Tamil/English
candidate labels, typed anatomical relationships and primary-source
references. All geometry keys and touch-target keys begin with
"pending-" and are intentionally **not drawing data**.

The native anatomy contract now prohibits acceptance of any plate
containing a pending geometry or touch-region key, even if review fields
are filled. Independent JVM tests cover this safeguard.

## Source-specific anatomical verification

- P. caudatum microscopic morphology, nuclear structures, ciliary
  metachrony and contractile-vacuole references:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10143506/
- Oral apparatus and cytostome/cytopharynx ultrastructure in
  P. caudatum, primary microscopic research:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2109358/
- Educational annotated whole mount of Paramecium sp. (use for
  terminology comparison, NOT species-specific proof):
  https://omeka.uottawa.ca/biodidac/items/show/4173
- Review of diverse contractile-vacuole systems:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38688044/

No figure, atlas scan or SVG was copied. The links are source registers,
not automatic approval. The open Tamil terms (vestibule, trichocysts,
collecting canals and basal bodies) need specialist review. Relationships
and anatomical orientation must be checked against the *P. caudatum*
reference, especially the oral depression and the collecting canals.
