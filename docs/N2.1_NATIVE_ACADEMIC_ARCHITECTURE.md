## New science/data integrity models

BilingualLessonContract requires unique sections, EN/TA headings and paragraphs,
source references for every section and separate science/Tamil review metadata
for ACCEPTED status. No real lesson prose is supplied by this phase.

AnatomySpecificationContract requires an organism/plate already listed in the
nine-study inventory, unique organ IDs, geometry keys and touch targets,
fully bilingual labels and optional bilingual narration. Dangling relationships
are rejected. ACCEPTED requires complete per-feature geometry review metadata.
These structural checks cannot independently determine scientific correctness.

Existing unresolved Earthworm, Penaeus, Pila and Asterias historical validation
issues remain OPEN until science, labelling and visual geometry are audited
against specific cited source pages. Do not import HTML/SVG as accepted native
Canvas. Tamil anatomical terminology requires bilingual subject review.
