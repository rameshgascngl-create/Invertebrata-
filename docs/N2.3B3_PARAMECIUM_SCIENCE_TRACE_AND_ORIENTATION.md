## Scientific findings cross-checked for Paramecium caudatum

**Species/authenticity and orientation.** The university UG teaching PDF below,
"External features", PDF page indexed 4 (viewer page 5), describes a rounded
anterior end and cone-shaped posterior end and an oral/ventral side. The
peer-reviewed "Integrative Neuroscience..." (Figure 1B, Jennings 1899a)
identifies P. caudatum and marks anterior (a), posterior (p), oral groove (g),
mouth, oral side (o), aboral side (ab) and contractile vacuole.

  real portrait/landscape sizes with TalkBack and zoom; record screenshots.

  considered for separate ACCEPTED status.
