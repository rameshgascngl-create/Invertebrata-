- Tamil terminology and several image labels need independent proofreading; do not copy generated lettering into final raster masters.
- Ciliary locomotion: check effective/recovery strokes, movement direction and the relationship between local ciliary beating and metachronal waves; a static arrow alone is insufficient.

- Conjugation: the plate must clearly distinguish temporary pairing, micronuclear meiosis, reciprocal exchange, fusion/reorganization and separation; conjugation itself does not immediately increase cell number.
- A multi-panel overview is not a substitute for individual, concept-specific PNGs in the relevant textbook sections.
