# N2.4 — Pragmatic illustration acceptance

Minor visual imperfections are not blockers. This supersedes aesthetic-perfection requirements but does not waive material biology errors, reuse rights, or Android accessibility.

| Decision | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ACCEPT_AS_IS | Recognisable correct anatomy, slight blur, amateur photograph, stylistic differences | Integrate original PNG; document its actual resolution and provenance; no artificial upscaling claims |
| ACCEPT_WITH_MINOR_FIX | Minor Tamil typo, reversible crop, one misplaced leader endpoint | Apply targeted correction once, then integrate |
| ACCEPT_AS_SCHEMATIC | Simplified but scientifically consistent, no false measured scale | Label schematic / விளக்கப்படம் and integrate |
| BLOCK_MATERIAL_ERROR | Wrong organ topology, nuclear ploidy/count, reproduction sequence or species | Correct or replace; keep explanatory text until then |
| RIGHTS_HOLD | Reuse licence or permission unresolved | Stage only; do not distribute in APK |

## Execution order

1. Inspect already committed or user-supplied PNGs before making new artwork.
2. Record path, SHA-256, chapter, species, visual decision, rights decision and material defects for each image.
3. Integrate accepted plates in the existing native Compose image viewer without repeated aesthetics-only reviews.
4. Check image routing, duplication, Tamil text, full-screen zoom and actual screenshots at 100% and 200% font scales.
5. Preserve all 43 validated chapters and approved assets. Keep independent physical-device and production-release gates.

## Paramecium guardrails

Existing fuzzy ciliary, oral and contractile-vacuole crops may be retained if their labelled structures are recognisable; do not misrepresent them as distinct high-resolution originals. They are not valid replacements for binary-fission or conjugation nuclear stages. Never show separated daughter cells before cytokinesis, label three haploid nuclei as 3n, or show mature new macronuclei before intervening postzygotic divisions. An inaccurate poster remains blocked even if visually attractive.

This policy does not retroactively approve uninspected plates. For each image separately track visual acceptance, rights clearance, Android integration, emulator evidence and physical-device acceptance. Production release remains HOLD.
