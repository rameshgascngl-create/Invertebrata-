# Paramecium caudatum — individual reproduction PNG acceptance brief

Status: **ARTWORK HOLD / NOT APPROVED**. This is a scientific production specification, **not** a declaration that new artwork exists. The Kotlin simulator currently has no approved PNG for `BINARY_FISSION` or `CONJUGATION`. Its seven-stage conjugation teaching text must not be mistaken for seven verified illustrations.

## Existing Library candidates inspected (30 October 2026)

1. **Paramecium Conjugation: Bilingual Scientific Poster.png** (eight-panel poster): reject for direct app use. Panel 7 depicts recognisable new MACs at pair separation without showing the intervening postzygotic divisions and differentiation; the lower nuclear summary labels three degenerating haploid products as **(3n)**, conflating *three nuclei each n* with *one triploid nucleus*. The summary jumps from one pronucleus to fusion, omitting the two pronuclei generated by the surviving haploid nucleus. The Tamil contains incorrect or garbled terms (e.g. பேருட்கரு/சிற்றுட்கரு replacements) and cannot be reused as label copy.
2. **Paramecium Binary Fission: Eight Stages.png**: reject for direct app use. Several Tamil anatomical labels are malformed, and the headline uses an unsuitable translation for transverse binary fission. The final caption says the two daughters are "genetically identical" without qualification; asexual daughters are normally clonal but mutation and genome processing mean an absolute identity claim is not warranted. The compact eight-column landscape layout is illegible on a phone. A clean individual PNG must be checked independently against the actual nucleus/cortex morphology.
3. Existing ciliary, oral and osmoregulatory PNGs are **three crops from one fuzzy source photograph**, not distinct high-resolution physiological plates. Do not repurpose them as reproduction images or present enlargement as restored detail.

Neither user-supplied artwork nor a published reference image is an automatic grant of source rights or scientific approval.

## Individual PNG production matrix

Each entry requires its **own** raster plate with a clearly named species (`P. caudatum`), native-size text legibility, a stable aspect ratio, a labelled morphology audit, a source record and separate English/Tamil approval. Do not create Canvas/vector fallbacks or reuse an unrelated plate.

### Transverse binary fission (vegetative, one original cell)

| Plate ID | Anatomical state and required endpoints |
| --- | --- |
| F01 | Single ciliated, polarized cell. Leader lines terminate on the **cell-boundary cilia**, large **MAC**, smaller **MIC**, and **ventral oral apparatus** respectively; not on the surrounding cytoplasm. |
| F02 | Replicated MIC chromosomes within the **intact MIC nuclear envelope**. A chromosome inset, if present, is marked *schematic, not species karyotype*. |
| F03 | MIC mitotic spindle and segregating chromatids; show **two nuclear complements**, but still **one cell boundary**. |
| F04 | Elongating/partitioning MAC (not a conventional chromosome mitosis). Separate leader lines distinguish the MAC and two MIC products. |
| F05 | Transverse furrow **crosses the anterior–posterior long axis**; the cytoplasmic bridge remains continuous. |
| F06 | Anterior **proter** and posterior **opisthe** separate; each has MAC + MIC and its appropriate cortical/oral complement. Two cells appear **only here**. |

### Conjugation (two compatible partners; species-specific nuclear sequence)

| Plate ID | Anatomical state and required endpoints |
| --- | --- |
| C01 | Two separate compatible cells A/B, oral-facing association. Label **two cell outlines**, MAC and MIC in **each**. A/B are not male/female. |
| C02 | MIC meiosis I and II: **four haploid products per partner**, while cell count remains two. Label *four nuclei (each n)*, never *3n* for three degenerating nuclei. |
| C03 | One selected paroral haploid product, **three other products degenerating**. Pointer ends at the surviving product near the **paroral region**, not the old MAC. |
| C04 | Survivor's haploid mitosis: **stationary** and **migratory** pronucleus in **each partner**, each n. Two distinct pointers per cell. |
| C05 | Reciprocal migratory-pronucleus exchange A↔B; two opposite arrows terminate in **the partner cells**, stationary pronuclei stay in place. |
| C06 | Fusion of incoming and stationary pronuclei: **one diploid synkaryon per cell** (n+n→2n), two cells still present. |
| C07 | **First postzygotic mitosis while the pair remains associated**: two diploid products per partner. Do not display newly mature MACs. |
| C08 | Pair separates **after the first synkaryon division** in the cited `P. caudatum` microscopy sequence. Two exconjugants, each with two diploid products; this order is not asserted universal to all Paramecium. |
| C09 | Two further postzygotic mitoses: four then **eight diploid products per exconjugant**. Distinguish four posterior **MAC anlagen** from four anterior presumptive **germline nuclei**; parental MAC fragments may persist. |
| C10 | Later new-MAC differentiation and **subsequent** cell fissions. Show cellular multiplication only at cytokinesis, not at pair separation or synkaryon division. Avoid claiming instantaneous disappearance of the old MAC. |

## Technical and scientific acceptance gates

1. **One PNG per actual state**, no 8-panel shrink-to-fit poster. Suggested target at least 1800 pixels on the long side, lossless PNG, and original master retained. Resolution alone does not establish biological accuracy.
2. At 100% and 200% Tamil system font scales, each figure and leader-line endpoint must remain visible, non-overlapping and independently readable in the full-screen zoom viewer. Verify screenshot **pixels**, not only Compose node existence.
3. No false scale bars, measured time claims, invented chromosome numbers, reused unrelated organ geometry or misleading stage numbering. MIC ploidy, number of nuclei and number of cells must remain distinct.
4. Commit a rights record for **every** image: creator, original URL or user-supplied provenance, licence/permission, attribution wording, transformations, access date and exact file hash. Citation is not a substitute for reuse permission.
5. Separate approval records: zoological anatomy, Tamil textbook register, source rights and physical-device accessibility. A green JVM/emulator run cannot set any of these to approved.
6. Preserve the 43 validated A5 chapters and existing PNG-only simulator route guard. Until all gates pass, `ParameciumProcessPngIndex` entries for both reproduction processes remain `null`.
