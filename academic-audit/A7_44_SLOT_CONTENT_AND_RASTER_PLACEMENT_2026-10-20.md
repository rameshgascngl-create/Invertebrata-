## Acceptance language

A chapter has a **native bilingual manuscript route** when English and Tamil explanatory sections are present in Kotlin. This is **not** proof of full biological accuracy, flawless Tamil, accessibility, verified anatomical depiction, image rights or device acceptance. The `DRAFT_UNVERIFIED` status must remain. The new `all44TeachingSlotsRetainSyllabusSpecificBiologyInBilingualText` test checks chapter-specific scientific terms for all 44 slots, rather than section counts alone.
