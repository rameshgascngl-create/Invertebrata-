# INVERTEBRATA v1.8.8 — Pencil Drawing Completion Gate

This is a targeted correction after illustrated-content QA found the existing v1.8.8 candidate incomplete. It does not redesign or shorten the UG theory.

## Visual standard
All new instructional plates must be local inline SVGs in a neat university Zoology record-book pencil style: white background, fine graphite-like linework, restrained anatomical shading, biologically correct proportions, clean leader lines or numbered callouts, uncluttered labels, responsive viewBox, and tap-to-enlarge compatibility. No cartoon styling, decorative backgrounds, surreal anatomy, photorealistic texture, or remote assets.

## Required major type-study plates

### Sycon
External morphology; longitudinal section; canal system; spicules.

### Obelia
Colony; hydranth; gonangium; medusa; life cycle.

### Fasciola hepatica
External morphology; digestive system; reproductive system; excretory system; life-cycle stages.

### Ascaris lumbricoides
Male and female external morphology; digestive system; male reproductive system; female reproductive system; transverse section.

### Earthworm
External morphology; digestive system; blood vascular system; nervous system; reproductive system; nephridia.

### Cockroach
External morphology; digestive system; respiratory system; circulatory system; nervous system; male reproductive system; female reproductive system.

### Penaeus / prawn
External morphology; appendages; digestive system; circulatory system; respiratory system; nervous system; reproductive system.

### Pila
Shell/external morphology; digestive system; respiratory structures; circulatory system; nervous system; reproductive system.

### Asterias
External morphology; water vascular system; digestive system; nervous system; reproductive system.

## Other syllabus organisms
Amoeba, Paramecium, Entamoeba, Trypanosoma, Leishmania, Plasmodium, Aurelia, Physalia, Taenia solium, Nereis, and every other organism discussed in the application must have an appropriate organism drawing and any specialised/life-cycle plate actually required by its lesson.

## Acceptance rule
A drawing is NOT complete merely because an SVG definition exists. It passes only when it is scientifically reviewed, visibly embedded beside the corresponding theory, has an accurate caption and labels, remains within the mobile reading viewport, enlarges correctly, is available offline, and passes physical-device QA in English/Tamil/bilingual presentation where applicable.
