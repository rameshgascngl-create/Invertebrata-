## Conversion rule
For each lesson, preserve the biological structures and organization visible in the corresponding draft plate, then redraw the needed teaching figure as a clean, simplified, monochrome pencil-style local SVG. Do not invent structures absent from the reference. Dense infographic text is not copied into the drawing. Existing theory text remains unchanged. Captions/legends are supplied by the app's language layer. Every SVG must use a responsive viewBox and remain compatible with the existing tap-to-enlarge viewer.
